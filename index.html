About

Two brothers, fifteen years, no shortcuts

Steve and Paul Whitlock trained as roofers with separate contractors around North Essex before pooling their tools and vans in 2011 to go out on their own.

Roofing is one trade where cold-call pressure and "storm chaser" cowboys are a well-known local problem. Colne Valley Roofing leans the other way: local, traceable, fully insured, and every re-roof comes with a written guarantee.
